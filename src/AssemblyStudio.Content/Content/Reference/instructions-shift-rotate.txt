Shifts and rotates
Generated from the instruction reference data and the engine's opcode table. Each flag statement is checked against the engine by the automated tests.
Flags: OF overflow, DF direction, IF interrupt enable, TF trap, SF sign, ZF zero, AF auxiliary carry, PF parity, CF carry.

SHL - Shift left (also SAL)
  Shifts the bits left; the top bit goes to CF and a 0 comes in at the bottom. Each shift multiplies by 2.
  Example: shl ax, 1
  Flags changed: OF SF ZF PF CF
  Undefined afterwards: AF
  All other flags keep their values.
  Shift by 1 or by CL. The flags above are for a shift by 1. A shift by CL = 0 changes no flags; for counts above 1, OF is undefined.
  Opcodes: D0h /4 (GRP2 rm8, 1); D1h /4 (GRP2 rm16, 1); D2h /4 (GRP2 rm8, CL); D3h /4 (GRP2 rm16, CL)

SHR - Shift right (unsigned)
  Shifts the bits right; the bottom bit goes to CF and a 0 comes in at the top. Each shift divides an unsigned number by 2.
  Example: shr ax, 1
  Flags changed: OF SF ZF PF CF
  Undefined afterwards: AF
  All other flags keep their values.
  For a shift by 1, OF is the original top bit. Counts as for SHL.
  Opcodes: D0h /5 (GRP2 rm8, 1); D1h /5 (GRP2 rm16, 1); D2h /5 (GRP2 rm8, CL); D3h /5 (GRP2 rm16, CL)

SAR - Shift right (signed)
  Shifts the bits right, copying the sign bit in at the top. Each shift divides a signed number by 2, rounding down.
  Example: sar ax, 1
  Flags changed: OF SF ZF PF CF
  Undefined afterwards: AF
  All other flags keep their values.
  For a shift by 1, OF is cleared. Counts as for SHL.
  Opcodes: D0h /7 (GRP2 rm8, 1); D1h /7 (GRP2 rm16, 1); D2h /7 (GRP2 rm8, CL); D3h /7 (GRP2 rm16, CL)

ROL - Rotate left
  Rotates the bits left; the top bit moves to the bottom and is also copied to CF.
  Example: rol al, 1
  Flags changed: OF CF
  All other flags keep their values.
  Rotates by 1 or by CL. For a rotate by 1, OF is set when the top bit changed. A rotate by CL = 0 changes no flags; for counts above 1, OF is undefined.
  Opcodes: D0h /0 (GRP2 rm8, 1); D1h /0 (GRP2 rm16, 1); D2h /0 (GRP2 rm8, CL); D3h /0 (GRP2 rm16, CL)

ROR - Rotate right
  Rotates the bits right; the bottom bit moves to the top and is also copied to CF.
  Example: ror al, 1
  Flags changed: OF CF
  All other flags keep their values.
  Counts and OF as for ROL.
  Opcodes: D0h /1 (GRP2 rm8, 1); D1h /1 (GRP2 rm16, 1); D2h /1 (GRP2 rm8, CL); D3h /1 (GRP2 rm16, CL)

RCL - Rotate left through carry
  Rotates left through CF: the top bit goes to CF and the old CF comes in at the bottom.
  Example: rcl dx, 1
  Flags changed: OF CF
  All other flags keep their values.
  Counts and OF as for ROL.
  Opcodes: D0h /2 (GRP2 rm8, 1); D1h /2 (GRP2 rm16, 1); D2h /2 (GRP2 rm8, CL); D3h /2 (GRP2 rm16, CL)

RCR - Rotate right through carry
  Rotates right through CF: the bottom bit goes to CF and the old CF comes in at the top.
  Example: rcr dx, 1
  Flags changed: OF CF
  All other flags keep their values.
  Counts and OF as for ROL.
  Opcodes: D0h /3 (GRP2 rm8, 1); D1h /3 (GRP2 rm16, 1); D2h /3 (GRP2 rm8, CL); D3h /3 (GRP2 rm16, CL)
