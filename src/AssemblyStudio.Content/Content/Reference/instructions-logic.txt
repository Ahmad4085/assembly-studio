Logic instructions
Generated from the instruction reference data and the engine's opcode table. Each flag statement is checked against the engine by the automated tests.
Flags: OF overflow, DF direction, IF interrupt enable, TF trap, SF sign, ZF zero, AF auxiliary carry, PF parity, CF carry.

AND - Logical AND
  Each result bit is 1 when both operand bits are 1.
  Example: and al, 0Fh
  Flags changed: OF SF ZF PF CF
  Undefined afterwards: AF
  All other flags keep their values.
  OF and CF are always cleared. Use it to clear bits: and al, 0Fh keeps the low four bits.
  Opcodes: 20h AND rm8, r8; 21h AND rm16, r16; 22h AND r8, rm8; 23h AND r16, rm16; 24h AND AL, imm8; 25h AND AX, imm16; 80h /4 (GRP1 rm8, imm8); 81h /4 (GRP1 rm16, imm16); 82h /4 (GRP1 rm8, imm8 (alias of 80)); 83h /4 (GRP1 rm16, imm8 (sign-ext))

OR - Logical OR
  Each result bit is 1 when either operand bit is 1.
  Example: or al, 20h
  Flags changed: OF SF ZF PF CF
  Undefined afterwards: AF
  All other flags keep their values.
  OF and CF are always cleared. Use it to set bits: or al, 20h makes an ASCII letter lower case.
  Opcodes: 08h OR rm8, r8; 09h OR rm16, r16; 0Ah OR r8, rm8; 0Bh OR r16, rm16; 0Ch OR AL, imm8; 0Dh OR AX, imm16; 80h /1 (GRP1 rm8, imm8); 81h /1 (GRP1 rm16, imm16); 82h /1 (GRP1 rm8, imm8 (alias of 80)); 83h /1 (GRP1 rm16, imm8 (sign-ext))

XOR - Logical exclusive OR
  Each result bit is 1 when the operand bits differ.
  Example: xor ax, ax
  Flags changed: OF SF ZF PF CF
  Undefined afterwards: AF
  All other flags keep their values.
  OF and CF are always cleared. xor ax, ax is a common way to set AX to 0.
  Opcodes: 30h XOR rm8, r8; 31h XOR rm16, r16; 32h XOR r8, rm8; 33h XOR r16, rm16; 34h XOR AL, imm8; 35h XOR AX, imm16; 80h /6 (GRP1 rm8, imm8); 81h /6 (GRP1 rm16, imm16); 82h /6 (GRP1 rm8, imm8 (alias of 80)); 83h /6 (GRP1 rm16, imm8 (sign-ext))

NOT - Logical NOT
  Inverts every bit (one's complement).
  Example: not al
  Flags changed: none
  All other flags keep their values.
  Opcodes: F6h /2 (GRP3 rm8); F7h /2 (GRP3 rm16)

TEST - Test bits
  ANDs the operands and sets the flags like AND, but keeps no result.
  Example: test al, 1
  Flags changed: OF SF ZF PF CF
  Undefined afterwards: AF
  All other flags keep their values.
  test al, 1 then jz jumps when AL is even.
  Opcodes: 84h TEST rm8, r8; 85h TEST rm16, r16; A8h TEST AL, imm8; A9h TEST AX, imm16; F6h /0 (GRP3 rm8); F7h /0 (GRP3 rm16)
